# Platform reference

Checked 16 September 2026. Reverify for current decisions. This is a dated
research reference, not an account eligibility certificate. Inspect the actual
project assets and account before making an eligibility assessment; preserve
clip-specific findings in that project's review documents.

## YouTube

Explained sports replays, critical reviews and substantial commentary can meet
the reused-content originality requirement. Copyright remains separate. Channel
review can reject repetitive or mass-produced content and can reject reused
content even when the creator has permission.

- [Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- [Fair use](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148?hl=en)
- [US Copyright Office: case-specific fair use factors](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html)
- [Gameplay commercial-use guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/138161?hl=en)
- [Native remix permissions and externally produced Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10623810?hl=en)

Current ads/Premium entry: 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualifying public
watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualifying public Shorts views in 90 days,
plus other requirements and channel approval. Expanded YPP's lower threshold
(500 subscribers, three public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 qualifying hours or
three million qualifying Shorts views) is not Shorts ad-revenue access.

- [YPP eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- [Expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en)
- [Shorts revenue rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- [Announced program changes: check effective dates](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009)

## Facebook

The March 2026 originality announcement expressly describes third-party footage
with an on-screen creator adding new information, analysis or substantial story
improvements. Mere action narration, passive reactions and minor edits are not
enough. This does not establish a universal ban on faceless videos; it also does
not certify any particular faceless third-party template.

- [Originality announcement, 13 March 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)
- [Copyright: permission, public domain and applicable exceptions; edits alone do not clear rights](https://www.facebook.com/help/308895412492789/?locale=en_US)
- [Partner monetization policies](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/169845596919485)
- [Content Monetization terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/content_monetization_terms)

The accessible 18 March 2026 announcement describes Facebook Content Monetization
as invite-only. Interest registration: mobile Professional Dashboard > Monetization
> Content Monetization. Do not recycle old in-stream-ad follower/watch-time
thresholds as a guaranteed route into this program.

- [Content Monetization access and qualified views](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/)
- [Music Guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/legal/music_guidelines)

Partner-policy, terms and music pages were not fully verified in this pass
because web access returned login/throttle responses. Account enrollment and
current country availability remain unverified. Copyright Help did load in the
review browser. Never substitute an Instagram article merely because its URL
is on facebook.com.

## Instagram

The Content Monetization Policies allow meaningful enhancements such as
commentary, parody or creative editing when assessing reproduced material.
They separately prohibit specified formats including static videos, slideshows,
looping videos and text montages. Do not infer that every analytical replay is a
prohibited loop; assess its role and construction. A repetitive source-loop
template is not a dependable monetization assumption.

- [Instagram Content Monetization Policies](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/2635536099905516/?locale=en_US)
- [Instagram/Threads copyright guidance](https://www.facebook.com/help/354736791367645/?locale=en_US)
- [Instagram Partner Monetization Policies](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/512371932629820)

Gifts currently require a professional account, age 18+, at least 500 followers,
policy compliance, agreement to the terms, payout setup and an eligible country.
India is listed. Payment comes from viewers' gifts, not a general promise of
payment per Reel view. Collaborative posts and primarily child-focused accounts
are excluded from Gifts under the retrieved page. Account access was not checked.

- [Current Gifts eligibility and mechanism](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/738469380549477/?locale=en_US)
- [Branded content and disclosure](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/616901995832907)

Content policy, Gifts and copyright articles were read directly in the browser.
Do not present the 2023/2024 bonus experiments as an available 2026 entitlement.
Subscriptions, branded partnerships and any bonus/ad offers have their own
conditions; verify the actual Professional Dashboard before projecting revenue.

## Concrete source route to evaluate

[Valve's Video Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/video_policy) expressly
permits videos using Valve content and YouTube Partner Program or similar
video-site monetization. It excludes selling/licensing the videos or charging
viewers for access, and does not clear third-party assets/music. Prefer original
recordings; it covers Valve content, not every Steam game or another uploader's
recording. Advertising permission does not automatically cover subscriptions,
sponsorship, footage resale or every other revenue mechanism.
Check the reviewed asset's access: an account offering subscriptions does not
by itself mean its publicly accessible gameplay video is behind a paywall.

Recommendation: independently cleared visuals/audio plus substantive, accurate
editing, then platform-specific program review. Licensing and originality remain
distinct; neither substitutes for account enrollment.
