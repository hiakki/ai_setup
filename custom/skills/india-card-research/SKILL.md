# Indian card research

## Establish the decision

Identify the exact issuer, product variant, network, credit versus debit type, and relevant period. Account tiers, metal/plastic versions, co-brands, and targeted lifetime-free offers can have different terms. Ask only for missing facts that materially change the result; otherwise label assumptions and continue.

For recommendations, use the user's natural spending, owned cards, payment channel and realistic redemption preference. Keep eligibility separate from approval probability. For debit cards, check linked-account tier, balance requirements and conditional benefits instead of importing credit-card assumptions.

## Evidence and freshness

- Verify material fees, earning rates, exclusions, caps and benefits against current official issuer product pages, MITC, KFS, reward-program terms and change notices. Use official network terms for network benefits, and RBI/NPCI sources for relevant regulatory or payment-network rules.
- Record the URL, document title, section/page when available, retrieval date, effective date, exact variant and supporting condition for each material claim. Opening a page is required; search snippets are discovery evidence only.
- Resolve conflicts by product applicability and effective date. A newer marketing page does not automatically supersede explicit terms. If unresolved, show both and mark the affected conclusion provisional.
- Blogs, forums and aggregators can locate leads but cannot establish issuer terms. An unavailable official source means unverified, not zero fees or no exclusions.
- Keep historical audits tied to terms effective on the transaction date. If historical terms or balances cannot be reconstructed, label a current-terms simulation instead of claiming actual missed rewards.

## Normalize terms before calculating

Capture the relevant subset of:

- Joining versus renewal fees, taxes, waiver threshold wording (`>` versus `>=`), eligible waiver spend and anniversary/calendar/statement-year window.
- Reward units per spend block, transaction-level rounding, base/accelerated earning, shared or separate caps, cap units and reset periods. Distinguish total multipliers from additional bonus multipliers.
- MCC exclusions and channel restrictions: UPI, EMI, wallets, rent, education, insurance, utilities, fuel, government payments, portals and international spend. Reward eligibility and waiver eligibility are separate rules.
- Redemption route, rupee value per point, redemption fees, minimum blocks, expiry, conversion limits and required cash component. Miles and catalogue values are assumptions until the intended redemption is priced.
- Milestones, reversals on refunds, activation/spend conditions, vouchers and renewal points. Do not presume milestone stacking or that renewal points survive a fee waiver.
- Lounge spend qualification and lookback period, primary/add-on/guest entitlement, domestic/international limits and booking conditions. Value benefits at expected usage, not advertised retail value.
- Forex markup, applicable taxes, DCC, surcharges and convenience fees when relevant. A surcharge waiver is distinct from earning rewards. Reverify tax assumptions rather than hardcoding them as permanent law.

## Calculate net value

Apply eligibility and per-transaction earning/rounding first, then the documented cap logic and period state. Convert reward units using the chosen redemption route. Subtract applicable transaction, portal and redemption costs.

For annual comparisons, show usable rewards and benefits minus the fee actually payable and relevant costs. If a waiver reduces that fee to zero, do not also add the same fee as a benefit. Show fee savings separately against an explicit paid-fee baseline if useful. Keep first-year offers separate from recurring value.

Do not add the entire annual milestone or waiver value to every transaction. Compare incremental progress and achievable outcomes using remaining natural spend and opportunity cost on other owned cards. Never recommend revolving debt or unnecessary purchases to recover a fee; include actual borrowing costs when they apply.

For historical audits, process transactions chronologically with separate actual-card and alternative-routing ledgers, including caps, refunds and resets. A greedy per-transaction winner is not proof of the best annual allocation. Label heuristic results, missing statement history, inferred MCCs and estimated earned rewards. Confirmed earned rewards require statement or issuer evidence.

## Deliver and validate

Use a compact comparison or recommendation with assumptions, practical net value, decisive restrictions, direct citations and verification dates. Separate verified facts, estimates and unresolved terms. Match detail to the question; a simple fee lookup does not need a full portfolio report.

Before changing calculation code, check representative arithmetic independently: spend just below/at/above an earning block, remaining-cap exhaustion, period reset, an excluded transaction and a refund where applicable. Test waiver threshold equality and prevent fee/benefit double counting. Use synthetic transactions; retain no credentials or unnecessary personal statement data.
